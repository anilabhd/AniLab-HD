## 🎌 About Anilab

Anilab is an anime-focused entertainment and discovery brand built for fans who love exploring new stories, characters, genres, movies, and series.

We focus on creating engaging digital experiences, anime-focused content, and resources that make discovering your next favorite anime easier.
